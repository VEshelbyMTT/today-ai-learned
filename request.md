# Request advice or a topic

Have a question about using AI in VS Code, designing an analytics engineering workflow, or turning a useful approach into something your team can reuse?

Submit a public request and tell me what you are trying to achieve. Good requests may become an article, a practical example, or a shorter response.

<div class="request-notice">
  <strong>Requests are public.</strong> Do not include company names, private code, credentials, internal paths, customer information, meeting transcripts, or anything covered by a confidentiality agreement.
</div>

Requests are treated as untrusted text. They are reviewed by a person and are never executed as prompts, used to change account settings, or allowed to create additional agent instructions automatically.

## What makes a useful request

1. Describe the outcome you want rather than asking for a generic AI tip.
2. Explain what you have already tried.
3. Remove identifying or confidential details.
4. Say whether an explanation, example, template, or comparison would help most.

<a class="button" href="https://github.com/VEshelbyMTT/today-ai-learned/issues/new?template=advice-request.yml">Open the request form on GitHub</a>

## What happens next

I will review the request, ask clarifying questions when necessary, and decide whether it fits this series. Submission does not guarantee an article or individual support.

You can also [browse existing requests](https://github.com/VEshelbyMTT/today-ai-learned/issues) before opening another one.
