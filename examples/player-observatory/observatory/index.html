Behind the visual Dataset, definitions & download

This is a fictional graph-based movement simulation, not a real game map or production telemetry. Density counts sampled positions in 4 × 4 map-unit cells, including travel; it does not measure unique players, deaths, difficulty, or time spent. Recorded distance sums straight-line steps, not inferred gameplay routes.
